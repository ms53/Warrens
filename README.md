# Warrens
### Outline
Warrens is a turn-based 4X strategy game inspired by [Sid Meier's Civilization series](https://civilization.2k.com/) 
that tells a story about an emerging society of rabbits. The player is tasked with leading their own customised herd throughout the ages from
a small warren to an expansive empire on a procedurally-generated world. This game will be built with C++ and the [SFML library](https://www.sfml-dev.org/)
### What makes this game stand out?
- The player is granted with a high degree of customisation with regards to the traits of their initial rabbit society, their cultural traditions and their overall focus.
- The game attempts to craft a non-Eurocentric narrative of history by challenging the common assumptions about expansion and progression as pillars of civilization.
### What have we accomplished so far?
